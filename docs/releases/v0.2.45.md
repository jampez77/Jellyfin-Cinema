# ScreenHarbour 0.2.45 — prerelease

Large seasonal rows now use fewer graphics layers. Closed doors no longer retain 3D perspective and backface rendering for every item; only the selected door and a door finishing its closing animation use them. Doors, shutters, curtains and numbered advent flaps retain their artwork and reveal behaviour, and poster titles retain the clearance added in 0.2.44.

This change addresses a measured rendering-budget problem found while investigating Home freezing on LG webOS when seasonal effects were enabled. A 60-item photographic door row created over 90 compositor layers in an isolated Chromium fixture, compared with roughly 20–24 when 3D rendering was limited to the active door. Those figures demonstrate the reduced workload; they are not measurements from the affected physical TV. Confirmation there is still needed.

Existing settings, collections, seasonal dates and advent unlock rules are preserved. No reconfiguration is needed. A row whose appearance was temporarily set to Normal can be given its chosen effects again after updating.

## Validation

- Typecheck, client build and all 246 unit tests passed.
- 55 targeted browser checks passed: three new 60-card compositor-budget checks plus 52 existing seasonal, advent, frame-fit, editor, rank and Home focus checks. One optional native-CSS fixture check was skipped because its external fixture was not configured.
- The 60-card checks compare small and large rows, verify Home becomes visible, preserve every card and its dimensions, exercise remote selection and closing, and check that completed transitions release their graphics layers.
- In the measured 1920×1080 Chromium fixture, photographic/nightmare doors fell from 104 idle / 105 focused compositor layers to 19 idle / 24 focused. Advent doors fell from 48 / 49 to 19 / 22. All decorative images remained present.
- All 700 server checks passed against Jellyfin 10.11.0. Packages build against all three supported targets. Archive verification checks embedded client and artwork, assembly identity/version, MD5/SHA-256 and preservation of historical catalogue entries.
- The installed 0.2.44.3 script was verified on the user's server, and the TV session was signing in successfully. No live settings were changed during diagnosis. Physical LG webOS recovery remains unconfirmed until this update is installed and the effects are retried.
