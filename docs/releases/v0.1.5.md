The TV Shows library now has the same browsing layout as Movies. This update also fixes the old Jellyfin controls showing beneath theme videos and reduces excessive cropping of guide artwork.

- **TV Shows:** All shows, Suggestions, Favourites and Genres, with search, A–Z/# filters, pagination and Collections. Returning from details preserves the filters and selected card.
- **TV suggestions:** Jellyfin’s Continue watching, Next up and Recently added lists. Episode cards include the series name, episode title and season/episode numbers, and open that specific episode in the show’s details.
- **Native page visibility:** hides Jellyfin’s original controls and descriptions while our layout is open, including pages inserted or reused later. Native Play, Trailer and theme-video actions remain available. The original page is restored when leaving the layout.
- **TV guide artwork:** preserves image proportions on the right, shows more of the composition, and stays clipped above the schedule. Stronger sizing rules prevent native styles from stretching it. Channel-logo fallback remains contained.
