# Jellyfin Cinema 0.2.21 — prerelease

Home no longer stays blank because an unrelated Jellyfin spinner still has its active class. Readiness follows Home's native rows and their actual completion callbacks. Native, streaming-service and collection rows still appear together, including when Jellyfin rebuilds Home on Back.

Returning Home also stops waiting for Featured to fetch its carousel and preload artwork again. Featured keeps its own reserved space and completes normally. The initial Home visit retains the cinema animation and waits for Featured as before.

Cached rows paint before background settings and collection refreshes begin. Collection revalidation runs at most two member requests at once, shares an existing batch when focus/visibility changes, and stops starting queued requests after leaving Home or switching accounts. Settings sync, collection updates, selection and scroll restoration remain supported.

## Installation

Update from the existing Cinema catalogue, restart Jellyfin, then reload desktop Jellyfin and fully close/reopen the TV app.

## Validation

- TypeScript, all 176 unit/API tests, 99 affected browser tests and the server verification checks passed. Packages build successfully for all three supported Jellyfin targets.
- Live diagnosis on the installed 0.2.20 client confirmed a hidden active spinner held a ready Home until the 3.5-second fallback. Temporarily removing that false signal in an isolated browser tab reduced the observed blank period from 3.53 seconds to 0.73 seconds. This was a diagnostic comparison, not a physical deployment of 0.2.21 or a guaranteed load time.
- Regression coverage includes delayed native rows, a lingering global spinner, the real upstream Featured client with a held repeat response, background refresh concurrency, account isolation, and focus/scroll preservation.
- The new build has not been installed or tested on the physical LG TV during development.
