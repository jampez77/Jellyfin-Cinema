# ScreenHarbour 0.2.30 — prerelease

Trailer buttons now sit **just above Jellyfin’s playback controls**. Their position follows the actual control area, including different TV font sizes and themes, instead of a fixed distance from the bottom. They stay in place while the native controls fade away.

Pause-screen descriptions fit into the space above the buttons and **fade transparently at the bottom when clipped**. Short descriptions remain fully visible. Watchlist feedback appears above the button row, keeping it clear of playback controls.

Initial **Skip trailer** focus, the first-playback queue fix, paused trailer actions, TV Browse-icon suppression and autoplay after Skip are retained.

## Update

Update **ScreenHarbour**, restart Jellyfin, then fully reload the browser or close and reopen the web-based TV client. Keep **File Transformation** enabled. Saved settings and plugin identity are preserved.

## Validation

45 browser checks passed across trailer actions, initial loading, pause screens and the demo. Geometry checks cover 1440×900, 1920×1080, 1280×720 and 1280×600 with native and ElegantFin styling, populated native title/metadata, large playback-control fonts and multiline pause titles. Checks cover the measured gap, faded and hidden controls, long-description fade, short-description clarity, resizing, initial focus, saving and skip autoplay. TypeScript typecheck and production builds passed.

Browser media transport is simulated. This package has not been installed on the production server or tested on a physical TV.
