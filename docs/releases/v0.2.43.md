# ScreenHarbour 0.2.43 — prerelease

**A film behind every numbered advent-calendar door.**

Christmas seasonal collection-item rows now offer **Advent calendar doors**: a single festive flap with its own number, perforated edges and a pull notch. Choose **Illustrated**, **Playful / family**, or **Gilded winter wood**; the colours and festive motifs vary across the doors. Numbers appear on the doors even when the separate Ranked artwork option is off.

In **Collections → Customize Home rows**, edit a seasonal collection-item row, choose **Christmas** in **Appearance**, then select **Advent calendar doors** under **Item reveal**.

- **Daily from season start** opens door 1 on the seasonal row’s start date, door 2 the following local day, and so on. Set the start date to **1 December** for a traditional advent calendar. Future doors stay closed, conceal the film title and show their opening date. Earlier doors remain available.
- **Open any door on focus** makes every door available whenever it is focused or hovered, without a date restriction.

An available door swings open when focused or hovered and closes when you move away. Selecting it opens the film’s normal details. The editor preview always allows every door to open, including outside the configured season.

Door numbers follow the displayed item order. Daily calendars ignore **Shuffle on load** while retaining that saved preference for other reveal modes. Choose the collection and arrange films in **Item order**; keep the collection and ordering fixed during the calendar if each film should retain its day. Each collection tab has its own numbered sequence. A calendar changes access from this Home row only; the films remain available through the rest of Jellyfin.

Dates repeat annually, use the viewing device’s local calendar and support seasons crossing New Year. Open Home rows unlock in place at midnight or after the client wakes, preserving focus and scroll position. Out-of-season rows retain their existing visibility rules. Reduced-motion preferences disable the flap transition.

Existing rows keep their current reveal mode. New advent settings sync with the seasonal row; older clients may read them but cannot overwrite settings they do not understand.
