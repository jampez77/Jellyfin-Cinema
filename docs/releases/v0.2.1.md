# Jellyfin Cinema 0.2.1 — prepared, not published

This update refines Home collection editing and brings music playback, playlists and recording controls into the Cinema theme.

## Changes

- The video preview trigger is a compact icon in the existing player overlay. Its “Episodes & seasons” label is accessible as a tooltip and to assistive technology, and no longer takes space from the native player controls. Mouse access and Down navigation remain available.
- **Customize collection rows** moves from Home to the Collections page. A row list and one editing workspace replace the stack of forms. **Content**, **Item order** and **Home position** keep each task separate, with Save and Cancel always available.
- Item rows can follow collection order, sort by title or year, or use a manual order. Ranked artwork follows that chosen order. These preferences apply to the Home row on this account/device; they do not change the shared collection.
- Custom rows can sit before or between native Home rows, including Featured and individual Latest library rows. Position controls show the combined order. Native rows retain their event handlers and preferences; custom rows reattach after native refreshes. If an insertion point is unavailable, the row appears at the bottom.
- Existing saved Home rows retain their selection, ranking and order. Until edited, they remain at the bottom and use the collection's existing item order.
- Album Play controls have enough space for their focus outline. The native music player and Now playing queue use Cinema colours and focus styling.
- Music Playlists has a themed list and track page. Whole-playlist and selected-track actions pass the complete ordered queue to Jellyfin, including repeated entries. Search, favourites, pagination, retry and Back focus are supported.
- Recordings, Schedule, Series recordings, series details and recording dialogs share the theme. Jellyfin retains scheduling actions, permissions, confirmations and playback behavior.

The plugin GUID, assembly, routes and archive names are unchanged. Build packages with `bash scripts/package-plugin.sh all`. The published catalogue remains on 0.2.0 until these packages are released.

Home preferences remain local to this server/account/browser. Limits are 12 custom rows, 40 selected collections per collection row, 2,000 saved manual item positions, and 60 displayed members followed by **View full collection**. Clearing browser storage removes these preferences.
