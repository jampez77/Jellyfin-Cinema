# ScreenHarbour 0.2.44 — prerelease

Seasonal frames now leave space around the whole poster, so film titles printed near its edges stay visible. Artwork fits inside the frame opening without cropping or stretching. Clearance follows the frame shape, including arched Halloween frames.

The fix applies to illustrated, playful, photographic and nightmare frames, on Home, ranked rows and the editor preview. Frames, doors, advent numbers and focus outlines retain their existing size. Rows without a frame retain their normal artwork layout. Existing settings are preserved; no reconfiguration is needed.

## Update

Update **ScreenHarbour**, restart Jellyfin, then fully reload browsers or close and reopen web-based TV clients. Keep **File Transformation** enabled.
