# ScreenHarbour 0.2.41 — prerelease

**Photographic seasonal frames now fit the item edges.**

Seasonal frames previously left visible space around some photographic styles, making the door surrounds look detached from the poster. The frame now fills the poster area, and the poster and closed door covers are clipped to the doorway opening, including its arched top. This keeps artwork from showing outside the frame.

Your saved scenery, frame, door and other appearance choices stay the same. Normal rows and illustrated styles retain their existing appearance. There are no new settings or configuration steps.

## Update

Update **ScreenHarbour**, restart Jellyfin, then fully reload the browser or close and reopen the web-based TV client. Keep **File Transformation** enabled. Existing Home preferences, collection rows, seasonal groups, provider settings, loading-screen choices and account settings are retained; no migration or reconfiguration is required.
