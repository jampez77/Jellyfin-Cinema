The TV layout now extends to Home, Music, Recordings and video playback. The guide tunes directly from its programme grid, and Movies and TV Shows open Suggestions first.

- **TV guide:** click/press OK on a live programme or channel name to tune that channel. Upcoming programmes continue to show details. Removed the top Watch live button and bottom channel-count/24-hour text.
- **Libraries:** Suggestions is the first, default view for Movies and TV Shows. Explicit native tabs and returning to a previous browse selection still work.
- **Home:** featured artwork, library navigation, Continue watching, Next up and latest additions, with native search, favourites and settings accessible.
- **Music:** albums, artists, album artists, songs, genres, suggestions, search, A–Z/# and favourites. Artist/album details open their real albums/tracks; playback uses Jellyfin’s native audio player.
- **Recordings:** active/completed filters, search, pagination and playback. Schedule and series-recording controls remain available on their native pages.
- **In-player browsing:** episodes across seasons, similar movies and live channels. Down opens the browser, Left/Right browses, OK plays/resumes, and Back closes. Current-device queue data resolves cinema intros, and switching waits for a changed local playback stream before closing.
- **Pause screen:** title/logo, metadata, synopsis and optional disc artwork, with season/series artwork fallback. Hides on resume, navigation and competing native dialogs.

The player integration follows [InPlayerEpisodePreview-TV](https://github.com/jampez77/InPlayerEpisodePreview-TV), with its MIT notices retained. Pause-screen behavior is independently implemented from [Jellyfin-PauseScreen](https://github.com/jampez77/Jellyfin-PauseScreen). Existing standalone versions take precedence to prevent duplicate controls; disable them to use the integrated versions.
