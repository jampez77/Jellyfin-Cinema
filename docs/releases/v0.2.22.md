# Jellyfin Cinema 0.2.22 — prerelease

Service pages, full row grids and settings no longer display data-source links, supplier labels or the Data credits panel. Content matching, chart order and availability updates continue as before. Source and asset provenance remain documented in the repository.

New and empty custom services now offer a **Collection** chooser immediately below their name and visibility settings. Choosing a collection adds the first row, fills in its name and previews its films and shows. Content controls appear before artwork and optional automatic catalogue settings. **Add collection row** starts another row at its collection chooser; names you edit are preserved.

Individual titles can be managed with **Add to collection** on their details page, then connected to the service by choosing that collection. **Refresh collections** reloads the choices without discarding unsaved service edits. Existing automatic services, catalogue IDs, collection assignments, account sync and the faster Home returns from 0.2.21 are preserved.

## Installation

Update from the existing Cinema catalogue, restart Jellyfin, then reload desktop Jellyfin and fully close/reopen the TV app.
