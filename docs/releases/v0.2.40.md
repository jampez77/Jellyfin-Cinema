# ScreenHarbour 0.2.40 — prerelease

**Give seasonal Home rows Halloween or Christmas scenery, themed frames and opening reveals.**

## Seasonal appearance

On desktop, open **Collections → Customize Home rows**, select a named sub-row inside a seasonal group, then open **Appearance**. Choose **Normal**, **Halloween** or **Christmas** and preview the result before saving.

- **Background:** none, static scenery behind scrolling items, or gently moving parallax scenery. The top and bottom blend into Home's background.
- **Height when focused:** standard, roomier (up to 1.5×) or immersive (up to 2×). A focused row reveals more scenery while posters keep their size. Growth is capped to fit the screen and closes when item focus leaves the row.
- **Artwork styles:** choose scenery, frames and doors/shutters independently from Illustrated, Playful / family or Photorealistic. Halloween also offers **Nightmare — very scary** for adult horror collections. Illustrated is the default; switching to Christmas removes Nightmare selections.
- **Themed item frames:** spooky window frames or festive advent-style decoration.
- **Item reveal:** always visible, opening doors, window shutters or drawing curtains. Artwork and titles are covered until the item is focused or hovered, then covered again when you move away. Christmas doors show item-position numbers; they do not lock items to calendar dates.

These choices work independently. The live preview uses the same focus and reveal behavior as Home, with a **Focus preview** button. **Save rows** applies the draft to the account; **Cancel** preserves the saved settings. Choose **Normal** to remove the decoration while retaining the row's content, dates, ordering and position.

Appearance is optional and limited to seasonal children. Existing rows stay normal until edited, only date-active children appear, and an inactive seasonal group leaves no heading or gap. Theme selection does not change season dates. Reduced-motion preferences disable animated transitions and parallax movement. Generated photorealistic and horror artwork is bundled locally with the plugin and demo; using it makes no requests to an external image service.

## Update

Update **ScreenHarbour**, restart Jellyfin, then fully reload the browser or close and reopen the web-based TV client. Keep **File Transformation** enabled. Existing Home preferences, collection rows, seasonal groups, provider settings, loading-screen choices and account settings are retained; no migration or reconfiguration is required.
