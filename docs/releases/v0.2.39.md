# ScreenHarbour 0.2.39 — prerelease

**Your saved loading-screen title now appears throughout the interface, including login.**

## Personal interface title

The **Title** in **Settings → ScreenHarbour → Loading screen** now replaces the wordmarks in the Settings section, loading editor, streaming-service editor, profile chooser and profile-switching screen. The Settings section uses your chosen title after saving. Existing saved titles apply automatically; there is no need to enter or save them again.

**Save** applies the title across the interface immediately. Unsaved edits and **Restore defaults** remain in the preview, and **Cancel** preserves the saved title. Leave the title blank to hide the wordmarks and their spacing. Long titles wrap, and custom text is displayed literally.

Signed-in titles use the current account's saved loading-screen settings and follow that account across devices. Before sign-in, login shows the last confirmed title this device used for the selected server. That local title survives a client restart and stays separate from other servers. A new device shows the default until an account has synced there. The settings endpoint remains authenticated; no public account-settings endpoint is added.

Cached titles display immediately. Background refreshes do not delay Home or its fast return visits, and late settings reads cannot replace a newer cached choice. Product names in update instructions, licences and plugin identification remain ScreenHarbour.
