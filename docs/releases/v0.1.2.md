Movie and series details now reveal Jellyfin's playing theme video behind the page. The highlighted programme in the Live TV guide has a hero banner, with artwork on the right and a fade behind the text.

- Reuses the native theme player and respects Jellyfin's settings. Static artwork returns when the video is unavailable.
- Falls back to the channel logo when programme artwork is missing or fails to load. The guide grid and navigation stay the same.
- Adds scrollable **More like this** recommendations to TV series pages.
- Adds **Collections** to movies and series that belong to a collection. Selecting a card opens that collection in Jellyfin.
